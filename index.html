About the park

Outdoor action, close to home

DFW Adventure Park offers paintball, airsoft, trap and skeet, sporting clays and team building for people who like their action outdoors.

The park covers 170+ acres of green space, creek-lined woods and winding trails, in the heart of the Dallas Fort Worth metroplex and one mile from Texas Motor Speedway.
